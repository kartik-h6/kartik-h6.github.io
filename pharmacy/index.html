Pharmacy

The B.Pharm programme, mapped as a structure — programme, semester, subject — rather than a folder of PDFs.

  • Semester 1

    Foundations — human anatomy and physiology, pharmaceutics, inorganic chemistry.

  • Semester 2

    Pharmaceutical organic chemistry, physical pharmaceutics, biochemistry.

  • Semester 3

    Pharmaceutical microbiology, medicinal chemistry, pharmaceutical engineering.

  • Semester 4

    Pharmacology, pharmacognosy, pharmaceutical analysis.

  • Semester 5

    Industrial pharmacy, pharmacology, formulation and dosage design.

  • Semester 6

    Medicinal chemistry, biopharmaceutics, quality assurance.

  • Semester 7

    Clinical pharmacy, regulatory affairs, elective streams.

  • Semester 8

    Practice-focused work, project and internship.

Semester themes follow the PCI B.Pharm framework at a high level. Subject lists and detailed notes are added as they are written — verify against your own regulation copy.
