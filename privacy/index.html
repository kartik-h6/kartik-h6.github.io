Privacy

What this site does and does not collect. Last updated —.

In short

This is a static website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and has no forms. Nothing you do here is tracked.

What is collected

  • Nothing automatically. No cookies, no local storage, no third-party scripts, no advertising.
  • What you send by email. If you write to me, I receive your address and your message, and use them only to reply.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (IP address, user agent) as part of running the service. That logging is GitHub's, governed by GitHub's own privacy statement, and is not accessible to me as the site owner.

External links

Links to other sites are provided for reference. Once you follow one, that site's privacy policy applies.

Your choices

Because this site holds no personal data about you, there is nothing here to access, correct or erase. For anything you have sent by email, you can ask me to delete it at any time.

Areas with their own terms

Some areas involve more than reading a page — a learning programme, a service, or a health tool. Where the data involved differs, the specifics are set out on that area's own page. This page is the site-wide baseline.
